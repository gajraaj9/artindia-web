# ===================== EN =====================

## Dates and place

Q: When is the festival?
A: Saturday 24 and Sunday 25 October 2026, from 12:00 to 22:30 each day.

Q: Where is it?
A: On the esplanade of Boulevard du Centenaire, 1020 Laeken, next to the Atomium. Entry only from Place du Centenaire.

Q: What is the festival?
A: The 10th Brussels Diwali Festival, organised by Art India: Indian music and dance on stage, food, stalls and fireworks. Theme: Culture United, Europe Celebrates Diwali.

## Tickets

Q: Why are there tickets this year? It was always free.
A: We want every visitor to enjoy it in comfort: a counted site so it never gets overcrowded, more space to eat and dance, a full stage programme and fireworks. A 10 EUR ticket, with children under 12 free, makes that possible. Buy early, bring the family, and be part of ten years of Diwali in Brussels.

Q: How much are tickets?
A: Presale 10 EUR until 30 September. At the gate: 15 EUR. Children under 12 enter free.
<!-- [1 OCT] A: 12 EUR online, 15 EUR at the gate. Children under 12 enter free. -->

Q: Where do I buy tickets?
A: Online at https://tickets.artindia.be. Payment by Bancontact, card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Revolut Pay.

Q: Can I buy at the entrance?
A: Yes, 15 EUR at the gate, card preferred.

Q: Do children need a ticket?
A: Children under 12 enter free but must be registered with a free child ticket when you book, so we can count everyone on site. Each child gets their own festival wristband.

Q: Is the ticket valid for both days?
A: Yes, one ticket lets you visit both days, as long as it is used by the same person.

Q: I did not receive my ticket.
A: Check your spam folder for an email from Ticket Tailor. If it is not there, write to diwali@artindia.be with the name used at purchase.

Q: Can I get a refund or change the date?
A: Tickets are not refundable. Your ticket is valid on both days, so no date change is needed.

Q: Do I need to print my ticket?
A: No. Show the QR code on your phone at the entrance.

## Lucky draw

Q: How does the lucky draw work?
A: Every adult ticket is one entry. You receive a personal link after buying; every friend who buys with your link adds one entry for you. Rules: https://diwali.artindia.be/draw. The email you received after buying explains how to qualify.

Q: What is the prize?
A: The prize will be announced in October.

Q: When is the draw?
A: Live on stage on Sunday 25 October. The winner is contacted by WhatsApp and email.

Q: What if I am not there at the time of the draw?
A: No problem. If your ticket wins and meets the other criteria, the prize is yours. The winner is contacted by WhatsApp and email.

Q: Where is my link?
A: Tap "My link" in this chat and the bot sends it again.

## Programme

Q: What is on stage?
A: Full programme: https://diwali.artindia.be

Q: When are the fireworks?
A: Around 21:00, subject to weather conditions. We will inform you of any change.

## Food and drink

Q: Is there food?
A: Yes, Indian food stalls on site, vegetarian, non-vegetarian and vegan options.

Q: How do I pay at the stalls?
A: Each stall takes its own payment. Cards are accepted everywhere; most stalls also take cash.

Q: Is there alcohol?
A: Yes, bars on site serve beer, wine, cocktails and soft drinks.

Q: Can I bring my own food or drinks?
A: No outside food or drinks, except baby food. Bags are checked at the entrance.

## Getting there

Q: How do I get there by public transport?
A: Metro line 6 to Heysel, then 5 minutes on foot. Trams 3, 7 and 9 also stop at Heysel. Walk towards Place du Centenaire.

Q: Is there parking?
A: Public paid parking at Kinepolis. We strongly recommend public transport.

Q: Is the site accessible for wheelchairs?
A: Yes, the esplanade is flat and paved, and there are accessible toilets.

## Practical

Q: What if it rains?
A: The festival takes place rain or shine; the stage is covered, the public area is open air. Bring a raincoat.

Q: Are dogs allowed?
A: No, for safety reasons, except assistance dogs. It would also be too loud for them.

Q: Can I re-enter after leaving?
A: Yes, keep your wristband on.

Q: Lost property?
A: Ask at the info point on site, or write to diwali@artindia.be after the festival.

Q: Can I perform, have a stall, or sponsor?
A: Write to diwali@artindia.be and the team will get back to you.

## Contact

Q: How do I reach a person?
A: Email diwali@artindia.be. We are very busy putting together a fantastic festival for you, so please be patient.
